### Interview guide (problem interviews)
1. Tell me about your role and a typical week.
2. Tell me about the last time you had to [job]. What happened?
3. What was hardest about it? What did it cost you?
4. What have you tried to solve it? Why did/didn't it work?
5. If you could wave a magic wand, what would change?
